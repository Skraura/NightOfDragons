# What's New in Beta 1.0 🎉

Welcome to the **first public release** of the DoD Dragon Tracker!

---

## 🔒 Role-based Access Control

Tabs are now gated by role:
- **Lineage** and **Feedback** are grayed out for regular members
- Admins and Devs keep full access to everything

The **Breeder clan role** is now an Admin/Dev-only assignment — regular members won't see it as an option when adding or editing dragons.

---

## 🥚 Smart Nesting — Mate Picker

When you select a mother or father in the Nesting Calculator, the other parent selector turns into a **smart popup**:
- Shows only dragons of the **same species**
- Shows only **opposite gender**
- **Excludes dragons that share a direct parent** (no inbreeding)
- Built-in search bar to find dragons quickly

---

## 🐣 Breeder Pairings (Admin/Dev only)

Admins and Devs now have a **Breeder Pairings** quick-load panel at the top of the Nesting Calculator, listing all compatible breeder pairs — click any pair to load them into the calculator instantly.
